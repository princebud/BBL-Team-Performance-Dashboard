# Main findings

Written by `data_processing.py` from the Big Bash League data. Re-run the script and this file updates.

- **Toss.** Toss winners won 43.2% of 37 decided matches. Teams that won the toss and chose to field won 33.3% of 18 matches; teams that won the toss and chose to bat won 52.6% of 19 matches.
- **Scoring.** Middle overs are the fastest phase. Runs per 100 legal balls: powerplay 206, middle 207, death 200. The death-overs rate was 203 in 2023/24 and 197 in 2024/25, with a peak of 203 in 2023/24.
- **Dismissals.** The most common ways to get out (retirements excluded): caught 100.0%.
- **Caveats.** 23 matches with no winner (no result, or tied without a winner recorded) are left out of win rates. Win rates are not adjusted for the strength of the opposition.
